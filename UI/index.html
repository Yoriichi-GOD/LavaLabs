    What to investigate next

    1. Verify transfer first. Compare unseen-site validation with the scored test result before using any indicator in a decision.
    2. Review missing observations. Check target label support and cloudy-image coverage before adding a model.
    3. Promote only a repeatable gain. Keep simpler controls when a feature family fails its second site partition.

    These are research verification priorities. The anonymous standardized targets do not provide facility locations, physical pollution units or a future forecast.
