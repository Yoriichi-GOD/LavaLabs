# Future held-out validation protocol

Apply only after the selected provider version and measurement files can be pinned from metadata and geometry-only access. The present run ends at the documented access and target blockers.

1. Choose the first eligible documented acquisition by a predeclared metadata rule and store the full object identity, revision, acquisition time, size, and license. Freeze the spatial envelope, output CRS, grid axes and target first.
2. For local direction, restrict to depth-averaged horizontal velocity and comparable acquisition geometry. Confirm direction frame and position uncertainty. Specify a geometry-only block/point assignment, speed and minimum-support threshold, and reproducible search neighborhood before reading velocities. Avoid choosing convenient cross-sections from measured performance.
3. Acquire and hash-persist nominal and alternative terrain separately. Check terrain age, native resolution, scene co-registration and declared coverage. Mark derivative hydrography and shared terrain as sensitivity sources. Use separate event-specific observations for endpoint exposure or full traversal.
4. Freeze the 45° diagnostic or justify a different physical tolerance from the new survey's support before opening labels. Specify the nominal, observability, conflict, strict, typed, abstention and ensemble comparison. Seal alternative configurations, ordered predictions, exact budget counts and tie rules.
5. Join independent reference outcomes once and retain all eligible, unavailable and unmatched units. Report native and matched coverage, correct retention, incorrect rejection, false rejection, and conditional agreement for each survey and condition. Leave deployment and pathway accuracy out of a local-direction comparison.
