# Independent direction and traversal reference search

12 November 2026. Metadata, methods and file headers were checked before any measurement-file access. This search downloaded no field files, opened no outcome columns, and produced no scores. The South Saskatchewan cohort of 42 previously scored blocks remains development evidence. An eligible new direction control would test dated, georeferenced, local horizontal depth-averaged current at its sampled support. Receptor exposure and directed source-to-receptor traversal require separately matched physical observations. [South Saskatchewan producer record](https://doi.org/10.5285/e4fe2ebe-b207-47d5-8c77-9873afc63da9)

## Decision

**Tanana is the first fresh local-direction candidate by target match. Its measurement files are named in official metadata and could not be retrieved in this search.** Both Copernicus comparison rasters and the MERIT-derived Alaska mosaic were reachable by header requests. The official data catalog identifies east and north depth-averaged velocity components and a compatible projected CRS. Two catalog snapshots disagree about the measurement schema and support count. The attachment URL, byte count and served version need verification before freezing a fresh evaluation. No matched physical receptor or source-to-receptor traversal label was identified across the four candidates.

## Eligibility before a fresh score

1. Pin the exact attachment and license, with size and hash, through an acquisition step that exposes only permitted metadata to the analyst. If the modern Tanana CSV is used, arrange a location-only record for independent support identification because the listed schema lacks a transect ID. Freeze the cohort and exclusions, the source lineage and terrain versions, the target scale and vector conventions, and the predictions before opening velocity components.
2. Transform coordinates into a common metric CRS and express measured and predicted vectors in a common heading frame. Account for true versus grid north and for unequal east–west and north–south ground distances in angular grids. Declare an alignment tolerance and a bearing sensitivity check from positional, smoothing and heading uncertainty. Receiver precision alone does not quantify alignment error.
3. Keep cross-sections or spatially separated blocks as units. Retain pass and within-transect observations as dependent support. Freeze a valid-speed criterion and define which reach widths, bank distances, raster support and co-registration residuals permit comparison. Keep weak currents, unresolved support and missing terrain cells in the eligibility ledger.
4. Report direction error and coverage only for the declared local support. Extending the test to an asset claim requires an observed event window, an identified receptor footprint and independent evidence of water at that footprint. A traversal claim additionally requires an observed, spatially and temporally coherent connection to the specified source. No such pair was qualified here.
