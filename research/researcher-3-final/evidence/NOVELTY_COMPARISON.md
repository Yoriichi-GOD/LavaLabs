# Novelty comparison for evidence qualification in flood screening

12 November 2026. This focused review compares decision behavior at the candidate, statement and reference levels. The source links below are primary publications, product documentation or standards. The accessible preview establishes the scope of Tan et al. Some implementation details remain unverified. The earlier audit covers a wider set of nearby methods. [Earlier novelty audit](../../../../../../cegs-novelty-20261112/NOVELTY_AUDIT.md)

## Contribution supported by the comparison

The Researcher 3 refinement specifies a flood-screening output contract. It preserves an existing proximity queue and qualifies statements on each retained candidate. A valid nominal modeled relationship remains available with its scope and typed limitations when alternate evidence changes the relationship or becomes unavailable. Evidence for physical water at a receptor and for directed traversal from a specified source is matched and evaluated separately, with documented reasons when the relationship, footprint, time, lineage or observational support fails to match. The scoped distinction is in this joint behavior on a fixed queue. The review does not establish algorithmic firstness, calibrated uncertainty, or an improvement in physical prediction.

The strongest direct threats are generic post-hoc rejection, operational ensemble and observation masking, and evidence-aware infrastructure inference. A scalar rejection policy on a frozen predictor can retain the same candidate queue. The comparison that matters is whether the richer contract distinguishes missing prerequisites, available but discordant alternatives, resolution failure, and target-matched physical evidence in ways that change an auditable statement at the same candidate and coverage.

## A runnable comparison against simple rejection

Use the same candidate IDs and ranks, nominal outputs, admissible observations, and fixed scenarios across arms. The existing [evidence-discrimination protocol](../experiments/evidence_discrimination/PROTOCOL.json) defines a nominal witness, strict gate, observability-only gate, observability-plus-independent-conflict gate, typed qualification, full abstention, and a directional ensemble-concentration comparator. For the Canadian direction control, the scalar comparator already defined in the protocol ranks complete cases by the length of their mean unit vector, with incomplete cases in a lower tie. This is a generic agreement-based rejection control. Neither GFM's pixel ensemble nor Geifman and El-Yaniv's calibrated selection guarantee is reproduced by that control.

For a new direction cohort, freeze the same nominal vector per unit and a reference-blind scalar score before evaluation. For complete units, use the mean resultant length of the normalized scenario vectors. Keep a declared lower tie for incomplete units, order only within the coverage analysis, and preserve the screening queue. At each coverage supported by both arms, take the selected top count and account for the full boundary tie. Compare circular absolute error and the prespecified wrong-direction rate against held-out ADCP. Report issued coverage among eligible units alongside the size of the full cohort, exclusions and affected positives where the target defines them. A second simple comparison can use observability plus a majority or agreement rule on available evidence. If used, specify the number of available worlds required and how missing worlds are handled. The comparator should retain the same nominal direction so the comparison isolates selection.

For road pathways, an implementable scalar comparator can rank positive nominal modeled witnesses by the fraction of predeclared pathway scenarios preserving the witness, counting an unavailable scenario in the fixed denominator. Freeze this scoring rule and its handling of ties before any new labels are read. Treat abstentions as unissued claims, retain the same candidate ranking, and do not fill an arm above its native issue set. Compare released modeled statements and limitations using controlled cases. Compare unsupported physical assertions and withheld verified positives only on a cohort with independent, target-matched endpoint or traversal labels, including justified positive and negative support. A model-world fraction is a sensitivity score and supplies no physical error denominator.

The held-out scoring reference must remain outside arm selection, parameter tuning and any in-arm corroboration. If a separate pre-release observation feeds the conflict arm, document its lineage and overlap with the held-out reference. Ahr and the 42 South Saskatchewan blocks are consumed development evidence. Ahr offers a fixed-queue and modeled-claim coverage comparison with zero matched independent physical endpoint or traversal references in the refinement protocol. The Canadian replay evaluates local heading on its existing blocks. Neither provides a fresh physical validation cohort. A tie, missing score or infeasible matched-coverage point belongs in the comparison record.

## Wording for the manuscript

> We preserve a fixed proximity-ranked flood-screening queue and qualify the statements attached to each candidate. We report a supported nominal modeled relationship with explicit limits when evidence alternatives are missing or change that relationship. We evaluate independent support for physical exposure at the receptor separately from support for directed traversal from the specified source.

The distinction is falsifiable. A fixed-queue scalar rejection policy could reproduce the same issue behavior at matched coverage. The proposed method then needs evidence that its typed outputs communicate meaningful decision differences or avoid specific claim errors on appropriately matched references. Current development evidence supports describing the implemented contract and testing its behavior.
