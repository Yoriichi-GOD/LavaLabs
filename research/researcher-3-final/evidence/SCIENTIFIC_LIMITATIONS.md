# Scientific limitations and decision boundaries

- **Held-out scope.** The original spectral protocol reserved four event crops and evaluated them once before these manuscript analyses. V2 visibility sensitivity and omissions reuse those crops. The final qualification pilot uses consumed Ahr and Canadian evidence. The software extension is a constructed contract test.
- **Spectral target.** Retrieval measures disagreement with curated total-water reference labels. The primary combined rule failed. NDVI alone is a prespecified secondary result, and ranking changes redistribute false-positive and false-negative disagreements. No measured human review time or corrected-map accuracy.
- **Reference-conditioned selection.** A benchmark visibility mask gives a more efficient review queue in these crops. The reference-blind replay verifies a ranking outcome under band validity. It supplies no operational cloud-screening performance.
- **Spatial and temporal support.** The optical pixel support, elevation surface, acquisition lag and unknown physical registration bound asset interpretations. A 30 m DSM and D8 relation cannot establish flow through narrow drains, culverts, bridges or sub-grid channels. A 30 m lookup displacement and 90 m reroute are sensitivity scenarios.
- **Ahr endpoints.** Forty-four explicit SpaceNet positives are interpreted water-or-rubble endpoints. There are zero eligible observed source-to-endpoint paths and no independently confirmed dry-road cohort. AP is benchmark-convention agreement. No physical pathway accuracy, calibrated error or physical exposure precision can be computed.
- **Canadian target.** Field ADCP is independent of the modeled vectors. The outcome is local measured-current angular agreement on one previously examined braided river. The angle tolerance is a diagnostic cut. Points and blocks share morphology and acquisition conditions. There is no fresh test, independent geographic replication or causal flow validation.
- **Matched coverage.** Tie expectations and exact feasible orders are combinatorial. They provide no sampling confidence interval. Strict CEGS has only two native directions, and the always-abstain control issues zero. Methods have no comparable conditional error where no statements are emitted.
- **Unsupported statements.** The 22 original and 15 extension cases test predefined software behavior. Real-world prevalence of these faults and errors outside the fixture design are unknown. A legitimate independent contradiction and an alternative modeled route are different evidence states.
- **Novelty and deployment.** The evaluated operation adapts established abstention, lineage and uncertainty ideas to a fixed infrastructure queue. It establishes implementation behavior and bounded diagnostic outcomes. Field accuracy gains, novel hydrology, transfer, calibration and analyst utility remain unproved.
