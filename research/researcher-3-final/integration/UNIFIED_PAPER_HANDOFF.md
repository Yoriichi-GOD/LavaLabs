## A. Five findings ranked by evidence quality

1. **Reserved spectral benchmark.** Four frozen event crops, 103,633 disagreements. The primary combined cue loses 13,637 at 20%. The prespecified NDVI-only arm gains 1,172 at 10% and 490 at 20%. Maintain both outcomes and specify eligibility.
2. **External water ablation.** In a separate six-window experiment the slope cutoff lowers micro IoU from 0.700446 to 0.596378 on 1,548,021 cells. The fixed terrain restriction can discard relevant true positives.
3. **Ahr endpoint ranking.** Proximity finds 38/44 explicit positives versus terrain's 22/44 and convention AP 0.737 versus 0.574 on the 199 supported roads. At 39 reviews, proximity finds 28 and terrain 25–26. The benchmark is positive-only in its explicit flood attributes.
4. **Qualified claims on a fixed queue.** All 60 IDs and priority ties stay fixed. Strict CEGS qualifies 3 of 23 nominal witnesses. Typed output describes the other 20 at nominal model scope. Software outcomes match 22 original and 15 new designed fixtures. Actual Ahr physical paths remain unlabelled.
5. **Matched local-current diagnostic.** One Canadian river, 42 consumed blocks. Strict, typed and ensemble selection tie at one agreement among two issued. Larger matched counts give no consistent advantage. Frame this as a negative or inconclusive diagnostic.

## C. Original research-question mapping

- **Change detection.** Complementary optical cues can reallocate existing errors for review. Same-acquisition cues and masked references limit the independence and scope of that evidence.
- **Spatial reasoning.** Terrain paths supply inspectable model relationships and expose sensitivity to terrain, alignment and scale. Proximity remains the strongest observed endpoint control in this cohort.
- **Exposure assessment.** Candidate nomination and physical consequence statements require different evidence. Retain the queue, qualify modeled pathways and require matched observation before reporting endpoint exposure or directed traversal.

## D. Scientific limits

Ahr lacks confirmed dry roads, matched directed observations and a measured physical registration residual. Copernicus DSM does not resolve culvert-scale dynamics. The reference may identify visible water or rubble. Canadian directions are local current observations from one previously evaluated river. The four reserve flood crops do not establish broad geographic consistency. Original and new faults are known-input checks. Human review speed, correctness of corrections, exposure precision, calibrated confidence and cross-region physical gains remain untested.
