# OBSCURA: optical spatial content, fusion, and new-site generalization

Research date: 2026-11-23. Read-only research; no datasets or weights downloaded, no installations, training, or account changes. Author source files were read as text. Reported paper results below are authors' results, not independently reproduced results.

## Decision

The literature supports testing whether **localized multispectral morphology adds information beyond global band summaries**. It does not establish that Sentinel-2 can recover OBSCURA's targets or beat the existing control. Keep the cheap sparse-hotspot feature ablation first. Reserve a tiny learned spatial branch for one subsequent mechanism test if the feature test leaves the question unresolved. Do not launch a general-purpose foundation-model search.

Expected OBSCURA gain: **unmeasured**. No defensible numerical estimate follows from these papers. The strongest reason to try an image branch is missing spatial representation; the strongest reason it might fail is the mismatch between an individually framed facility chip and a regional, background-subtracted atmospheric label.

## Input and target boundary

The following is the current task contract supplied by the parent investigation, not a separate audit of the dataset: 3,218 rows, 81 sites; 384×384 chips with 12 Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface-reflectance bands, SCL, cloud probability; 29 tabular variables including six weather quantities, terrain, indices, and cyclic month. Ground size and dates/year are withheld. Gas labels use a fixed 10 km window on gridded Sentinel-5P observations, subtracting the median of a 30–75 km background ring; observations are noisy and sampled over a few days. New test facilities are at least 150 km away and use disjoint periods. These are **column-window enhancement targets**, not surface concentration or facility emission rate. AAI is an aerosol index; it is not aerosol mass, smoke area, or gas concentration.

Three consequences are engineering inferences:

1. Crop fractions can represent relative context, but cannot be called 1 km/5 km scales. Resizing cannot recover missing geographic extent.
2. A source/activity proxy in the chip may explain part of a regional enhancement, but nearby sources, transport, and reference-ring variation remain outside the image. Better facility recognition alone may add little.
3. Atmospheric correction does not make visible plume morphology impossible: the smoke paper below used Level-2A. It also does not turn BOA reflectance into a direct atmospheric retrieval. UVAI uses UV scattering, while Sentinel-2's shortest supplied band is around 443 nm. [TROPOMI AI product](https://www.tropomi.eu/data-products/aerosol-index), [Sentinel-2 spectral bands](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s2-mission#S2Mission-SpectralResolution)

## 1. AQNet — Rowley and Karakuş, RSE 2023

**Paper → mechanism → prerequisites.** AQNet predicts 2018–2020 mean ground-station NO₂/O₃/PM₁₀ concentrations. It combines 12-band Sentinel-2 images, actual Sentinel-5P tropospheric NO₂ maps, and eight station attributes: altitude, population density, and six area/station-type indicators. The paper reports a random station split of 660/329/329 and an additional 36 British/Irish stations; the latter show overestimated aggregate air quality. For NO₂, its single-target model has MAE 3.72 versus 4.37 for the three-target model. This supports compact late fusion as a candidate, not an assumption that multitask learning helps. Neither pollutant-map inputs nor most station metadata exist in OBSCURA. The target averaging and geographic split also differ. [Full paper, §§3–4](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/159613/1/1-s2.0-S0034425723001608-main.pdf)

**Actual code.** The released model is MobileNetV3-small with a newly initialized 12-channel first convolution, a 640-dimensional S2 output, a 128-dimensional S5P CNN, and a 32-dimensional tabular MLP. It concatenates satellite features, then fuses tabular features. The passed `S5p_switch` does not select an S2-only model: both returned branches still require `s5p`. The data transform hardcodes a 200→120 center crop and band reordering; it must not be copied onto an unknown OBSCURA band order. Validation/test also use the shared randomizing transform in the released training entrypoint. These are research scripts, not a ready input-compatible library. [Model](https://github.com/CoDIS-Lab/AQNet/blob/3e7a951f83d93f68d92698025130b878c1a2e03d/Modules/SingleOutput/model_single.py), [transforms](https://github.com/CoDIS-Lab/AQNet/blob/3e7a951f83d93f68d92698025130b878c1a2e03d/Modules/SingleOutput/transforms_single.py), [training](https://github.com/CoDIS-Lab/AQNet/blob/3e7a951f83d93f68d92698025130b878c1a2e03d/Modules/SingleOutput/training_single.py)

**License and falsifiable test.** No license file was present in the recursive tree and GitHub's repository license field was null at review; public visibility is not a verified reuse license. Independently implement the mechanism. Compare a compact tabular MLP with the identical MLP plus image features, first on AAI alone. Their paired held-out-site difference tests the incremental image contribution without assuming beneficial cross-target sharing. [Repository snapshot](https://github.com/CoDIS-Lab/AQNet/tree/3e7a951f83d93f68d92698025130b878c1a2e03d)

## 2. Global-NO2-Estimation — Scheibenreif, Mommert, and Borth, TGRS 2022

**Paper → mechanism → prerequisites.** This work estimates ground-level NO₂, contrasting local and OpenStreetMap gradient boosting with S2/S5P deep fusion. The author record reports satellite-model MAE 5.50±0.14 µg/m³ and a US-west-coast out-of-distribution example. Its 14-page final PDF is marked restricted in the university repository, so this review does **not** claim to have read that final full text. The openly available 2021 predecessor was read: it uses spatial S2 context and S5P to supply changing atmospheric information at monthly/quarterly frequency. That temporal information is not an S2-only result and cannot be imported into OBSCURA. [Final author record](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/entities/publication/fb5ed843-8597-4cda-afdc-2bcd83e8148d), [2021 predecessor, methods and experiments](https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/climate-change-ai/papers/icml2021/23/paper.pdf)

**Actual code.** The final repository contains an S2-only route as well as two-stream fusion. S2 uses a 12-channel ResNet50 yielding 2,048 features; the S5P CNN yields 128, followed by a 544-unit head. Defaults load a BigEarthNet land-use checkpoint. The loader reuses one S2 image per station while S5P varies by month/quarter. Splitting groups by station at 60/20/20, not an explicit distance buffer or future-period split. Normalization statistics and image crop/band order are fixed to the source dataset. No `LICENSE` appeared in the recursive tree; GitHub license metadata was null. Dependencies include old PyTorch, geographic IO, and MLflow; importing the whole stack is unnecessary. [Model](https://github.com/HSG-AIML/Global-NO2-Estimation/blob/98271e3cd7563deb49ff345b0890c2e455e238fb/satellite_model/model.py), [loader](https://github.com/HSG-AIML/Global-NO2-Estimation/blob/98271e3cd7563deb49ff345b0890c2e455e238fb/satellite_model/utils.py), [split](https://github.com/HSG-AIML/Global-NO2-Estimation/blob/98271e3cd7563deb49ff345b0890c2e455e238fb/satellite_model/train_utils.py)

**Minimal test.** Separate static spatial context from weather interaction: pooled image features concatenated to tabular features versus the same image features multiplicatively gated by a small weather encoder. Use the same folds and training budget. Only the paired difference supports conditioning. Do not interpret a station-group split as verification of OBSCURA's joint spatial/temporal shift.

## 3. Characterization of Industrial Smoke Plumes — Mommert et al., NeurIPS climate workshop 2020

**Paper → mechanism → prerequisites.** This is the closest evidence that supplied L2A pixels can contain spatial activity information. Its 21,350 12-band, 120×120 images cover 624 industrial sites in 2019; all observations from a site stay in one 70/15/15 partition. Labels indicate visible smoke, not chemical composition. A modified ResNet50 classifies smoke at reported 94.3% accuracy; a U-Net uses 1,437 manual positive masks plus negatives, with reported IoU 0.608. Intermediate activations localize visible plumes, so global means can discard relevant sparse structure. But transparent smoke and surface confusion remain limitations. No result validates NO₂/CO/AAI regression, and OBSCURA lacks plume labels and known 1,200 m chip extent. [Full paper, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.11344)

**Actual code versus paper.** The paper says the classifier was trained from scratch; released `classification/model.py` calls `resnet50(pretrained=True)` before replacing the first convolution and head. Thus the released defaults are not a faithful scratch configuration. `data.py` selects 12 bands excluding B10, uses source-specific means/stds, random 90×90 crops, and rotations/flips. Training expects pre-separated folders: it does not itself enforce location grouping. Code is **GPL-3.0**. No weights are needed to learn from the mechanism. [Model](https://github.com/HSG-AIML/IndustrialSmokePlumeDetection/blob/3713196f209f8f73eeeb779407668a9257865185/classification/model.py), [data pipeline](https://github.com/HSG-AIML/IndustrialSmokePlumeDetection/blob/3713196f209f8f73eeeb779407668a9257865185/classification/data.py), [license](https://github.com/HSG-AIML/IndustrialSmokePlumeDetection/blob/3713196f209f8f73eeeb779407668a9257865185/LICENSE)

**Minimal test.** Compare learned spatial pooling against both the existing global summaries and a patch-scrambled control with identical per-pixel spectra. If scrambling preserves performance, the branch has not established useful morphology. Keep the facility center approximately fixed; crop-away-source augmentation would change the prediction problem.

## 4. Pollution tracker — industrial aerosol-source imagery, Environmental Data Science 2023

**Paper → mechanism → prerequisites.** This model classifies coal/steel/nonindustry sites, rather than aerosol abundance. It uses 7,829 RGB+NIR S2 patches and a 4-band ResNet50v2, with center 140→random 120 crops from larger source patches. Training from scratch outperformed ImageNet initialization. A Bing high-resolution second stage is outside OBSCURA's inputs. Its instructive finding is deployment failure: coal recall fell from 0.95 in held-out testing to 24/51=0.47 in a regional deployment even for plants present in the training set. Off-center/cut/cloud-covered scenes and real-world prevalence mattered. This is evidence to test framing/cloud robustness, not a transferable pollution-regression performance claim. [Full article, §§2–4](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/environmental-data-science/article/pollution-tracker-finding-industrial-sources-of-aerosol-emission-in-satellite-imagery/22F4760D7DF02B9243F0476371E31D4F)

**Actual code.** The notebook uses 4→3 channel 5×5 convolution with `tanh`, then `ResNet50V2(weights=None)`, flatten, and a three-logit head. Its source input shape is 230 pixels, a small discrepancy from the paper's 240-pixel extraction description. It contains multiple experiment cells and local paths; deployment loads a separately saved model. Dependencies include TensorFlow/Keras and geographic acquisition tools, not an inference-only package. Code is **MIT**. [Training notebook](https://github.com/ManshaP/sentinel_industry/blob/974e8d1e854de270f9b4716bbb8507c8cbefe288/train_res50v2.ipynb), [deployment](https://github.com/ManshaP/sentinel_industry/blob/974e8d1e854de270f9b4716bbb8507c8cbefe288/deploy_script.py), [license](https://github.com/ManshaP/sentinel_industry/blob/974e8d1e854de270f9b4716bbb8507c8cbefe288/LICENSE)

**Minimal test.** Evaluate small center translations and crop-fraction changes on held-out chips. Require stable aggregate results and inspect cloud-probability strata. Sensitivity to framing is a failure signal before adding capacity.

## Mechanistic fallback: one small spatial fusion branch

This is an **engineering proposal**, not an architecture proven by the cited papers. It uses only the provided inputs and can be implemented in ordinary PyTorch, without external training data or pretrained weights.

- Input: declared band order, reflectances divided by 10,000 under the dataset contract; training-fold-only normalization. Retain SCL as categories/quality indicators and cloud probability as reliability, not as additional reflectance bands. Hard-mask no-data/saturated-invalid pixels; report a cloud-only control before interpreting a quality-channel gain as atmosphere content.
- Two views: full 384 chip and center 192 crop, each resized to 96×96. Shared 12-band encoder with three small depthwise-separable convolution blocks (widths 16/32/64), GroupNorm, and smooth nonlinearity. Two views preserve both local source detail and relative context while avoiding a claimed metric scale.
- Pool: valid-weighted mean plus top-5%-activation mean per feature channel. Concatenate the two views, compress to 16 dimensions. This represents sparse structure rather than training an unlabelled plume segmenter. Aim below 100k trainable parameters; verify the actual count and memory before fitting. T4 runtime/throughput is not measured.
- Fusion: 16 image features plus a small 29-variable MLP. An optional 16-dimensional gate from weather multiplies image features before the same small regression head. Compare it against ordinary concatenation only after image value is demonstrated. First target AAI alone; missing values masked. No five-target loss or uncertainty head in the first experiment.
- Generalization: sample sites uniformly, then rows within site, rather than allowing long site time series to dominate. GroupNorm avoids batch-statistic dependence on which facilities share a small batch. These are proposed controls, not proven domain-generalization improvements.

Wind direction is **FROM**: if physical east/north components are needed, `u=-speed*sin(theta)`, `v=-speed*cos(theta)` with clockwise-from-north degrees. However, do not add wind-aligned spatial pooling or rotate weather vectors with pixels until chip orientation is established from allowed metadata. Randomly rotating imagery while retaining directional physics can create inconsistent examples. The first mechanism test can use speed/weather in the tabular branch without claiming a downwind image sector.

## Falsifiable experiment and stop rule

1. Freeze the established 64-site, 3×3 fold definitions and control predictions. Use the existing official masked score, exact target denominators, and accepted improvement threshold; no threshold is invented here. An inner site split chooses epochs; outer held-out sites cannot select epochs, features, or blend weights.
2. Finish the proposed cheap sparse-hotspot feature ablation first. If it closes the spatial question adequately, stop. If a learned-structure question remains, fit the small AAI branch with a matched tabular-only neural control, then a spatially scrambled image control. All controls use the same normalization, folds, random seed, and compute budget.
3. Compare both AAI and the **full official multi-target score** after substituting only AAI. Keep other target predictions frozen. Select any shrinkage/blend with the control inside training folds; never tune on outer held-out predictions. Training a residual on an overfit baseline's in-sample predictions would be misleading; if residual learning is used, generate the residual with inner-group out-of-fold predictions.
4. Require improvement beyond the existing threshold across repeated splits, stability by cloud fraction, and evidence stronger than a few favorable sites. Site-block bootstrap intervals are descriptive of these folds, not a guarantee for distant future facilities. If image and scrambled controls tie, or gains vanish under a second seed, stop the branch. Do not answer a negative result by upgrading to a larger backbone.

## Direct retrieval route explicitly ruled out

The primary NO₂ plume retrieval paper compares **top-of-atmosphere** blue/ultrablue reflectance against same-location plume-free reference passes, with wind information and scene screening. This is a different task and requires missing inputs. OBSCURA's isolated L2A chips cannot reproduce that pipeline; reference reconstruction is also outside the stated rules. [Methods: NO₂ column retrievals](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2317077121)

## What is proved, assumed, and untested

- Proved by this review: author code contains the mechanisms/dimensions above; the smoke paper uses L2A with site-held-out visible-smoke labels; the AQNet/Global routes use actual S5P information absent here; source licenses/default discrepancies were checked.
- Assumed for experiment design: enough spatial activity variation survives chip framing and L2A processing to add held-out-site information beyond the 54 summaries. The coarse regional target makes this uncertain.
- Untested: every OBSCURA gain, T4 throughput, split robustness, target-specific benefit, and whether learned spatial pooling beats the cheaper feature ablation. No reviewed model was run.
