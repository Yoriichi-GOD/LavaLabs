# Researcher 3 final findings

**Outcome B for the implemented contract. Outcome D for physical pathway validation.** The matched local-direction diagnostic does not establish a consistent improvement. Retain the typed qualification as a research mechanism and preserve the simple observability control.

| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Q1. Identify incorrect physical reasoning better than simple abstention? | The 15 software fixtures verify useful releases with appropriate withholding, including rejection of matched contradictions. Ahr supplies zero route labels. On the consumed Canadian local-angle target, abstention rejects all 29 angular disagreements and all 13 agreements. Its conditional error is undefined. |
| Q2. Retain more independently correct claims at comparable error? | No consistent gain. At two issued, B, E and G each retain one correct nominal direction and reject 28 disagreements, with one issued disagreement and 12 false rejections. At seven, E retains 2.67 expected and G 3. At 14, E expects 5 with a feasible range of 3–6 and G retains 4. |
| Q3. Which conditions were informative? | Missing necessary acquisition or receptor identity affects claim availability. Independent target, lineage, time and support checks decide what can be corroborated. The Canadian strata yield 1/2, 5/15 and 7/25 angular agreements. Their separation is weak and coverage limited. |
| Q4. Which disagreements reflect scale, position or processing? | Ahr no-fill retains 11/23 compatible witnesses. West shift retains 10, changes four and loses nine. Filled 90 m retains 1/23 at a 90 m path budget. These are sensitivities. Actual registration and physical pathway identity remain unknown. |
| Q5. Did additional evidence worsen decisions? | The frozen combined spectral rule and fixed slope restriction worsen their defined outcomes. Hard routing qualification removes 35 previously retrieved positive endpoints. The Canadian strict gate removes 12 of 13 nominal agreements along with 28 of 29 disagreements. |
| Q6. What can Canada establish? | Local depth-averaged ADCP angle agreement on 42 spatially related consumed blocks in one river. There is no fresh test, no event-level transfer and no measurement of complete traversal or asset exposure. |
| Q7. Does preserving the proximity queue improve retrieval? | The same IDs and scores guarantee the same 38/44 positives. The original evidence-first reordering had no meaningful advantage at matched road budgets. |
| Q8. What is the distinct behavior? | A fixed ranking plus separate releases for nominal model scope, endpoint exposure and directed traversal. Reason codes distinguish model sensitivity, missing input, unresolved scale and matched physical contradiction. This is implemented and fixture-tested. |
| Q9. Original hypotheses? | H1, terrain improves physical exposure over imagery, remains blocked. H2, multi-temporal urban evidence improves relevant change identification, remains partial outside the R3 paired observations and earlier urban work. H3, source–pathway–receptor reasoning improves exposure beyond proximity, is contradicted for the frozen Ahr endpoint benchmark comparison of these rules and blocked for physical generalization. H4, traceability reduces unsupported conclusions, is supported only for the controlled fixtures. Human explanation and decision quality remain open. The R3 primary combined-index hypothesis failed, and its prespecified NDVI secondary comparison gained modestly on the four crops. |
| Q10. What must the paper avoid claiming? | Better physical accuracy, confirmed Ahr flow paths, dry-road precision, calibrated confidence, independent geographic replication from Canadian blocks, operator invention, analyst time savings, or pooled “system accuracy.” |
