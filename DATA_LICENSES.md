# Data and asset credits

The MIT license applies to project software. The original evidence manifests identify imagery products, acquisition dates and checksums. The following credits apply to the public demo and to retained local research as indicated by each source report.

| Resource | Use and credit |
| --- | --- |
| [NASA POWER / MERRA-2](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_meteorology/) | Retained monthly model data for NASA trends. Source citations and exact response hashes are available in the app. |
| [Acker et al. (2009), NASA NTRS 20090042747](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20090042747) | Challenge-listed methodological resource: calendar-month climatology, monthly and annual departures, and ordinary/robust slope comparison in the Nepal investigation. Huber RLM is our stated adaptation. NTRS marks this NASA work as public use permitted. The paper's SeaWiFS data were not used. |
| [NASA JPL NASADEM HGT v001](https://doi.org/10.5067/MEaSUREs/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001) | Terrain source for the Kedarnath derivatives. |
| [USGS Landsat Collection 2](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2) | Kedarnath 2013 surface reflectance and quality flags. |
| [Copernicus DEM](https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/) | GLO-30 derivatives and historical GLO-90 Nepal scene. Produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-90/30 © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA. All rights reserved under the applicable product license. |
| [Copernicus Sentinel](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions) | Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023, 2024 and 2026, including radar evidence and retained RGB/index inputs. |
| [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) | Nepal receptors and historical infrastructure. © OpenStreetMap contributors, under ODbL. The archive includes the derived Nepal database and provenance. |
| [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) | Public domain land polygons in the NASA geographic comparison. |
| [Plotly.js](https://github.com/plotly/plotly.js/blob/master/LICENSE) | Bundled library under the included MIT notice. Geist, Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono carry the included OFL notices. |

Prior research source reports cite development reference data separately, including WorldFloods, MERIT Hydro, Canadian ADCP, SpaceNet, local urban inventories and the Rasuwa 2 m surface. The local source archive retains these inputs subject to their terms. The public bundle excludes native reference acquisitions and exact Delhi agency line geometry. Numerical summaries retain their original scope and interpretation. Scientific Noir decorative art is supplied prior project artwork. The team-supplied volcano wordmark and the Codex-authored fallback display visible SI labels.
